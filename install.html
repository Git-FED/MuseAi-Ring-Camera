MuseAi-Ring / Install
Hardware setup

Install first. Attach Muse second.

The attachment step only works after the camera is alive, paired, and current in the Ring app. Skipping that order is how a simple setup turns into an authentication scavenger hunt.

Battery install · about 10 minutes

  1. Charge the internal lithium-ion battery.
  2. Pair the doorbell in the Ring app and connect it to Wi-Fi.
  3. Mount the plate and doorbell where the camera has the view you need.
  4. Finish firmware updates and test a motion event and a doorbell press.
  5. Optionally connect existing wiring if the installation supports it.

Wired install · about 15 minutes

  1. Confirm compatible 8–24 VAC doorbell wiring and transformer capacity up to 40VA max.
  2. Turn off power before touching the old doorbell wiring.
  3. Mount the plate, connect the wires, and restore power.
  4. Pair in the Ring app and complete firmware updates.
  5. Test fresh snapshots, motion, and doorbell events before attachment.
Do not skip

Prerequisite

Your Ring account must already be connected, and the device must be initialized and firmware-updated in the Ring app.

Attach Muse is step two. It is not the activation step.

Before you blame Muse, make Ring prove it works.

Trigger a motion event, press the doorbell, request a snapshot, and confirm a recorded clip exists if you paid for a recording plan. These four checks separate device setup from connector setup.
