MuseAi-Ring / Future
What may change

The future has a door. It is not unlocked.

Ring’s 2026 partner direction changes the absolute story, but not the practical story for a small template. Official live video may exist for vetted partner apps while remaining unavailable to a lightweight, unpartnered wrapper.

A

Partner API, on paper

A gated Ring Partner API can support WebRTC/WHEP live video and historical MP4 downloads. It is a potential official path—not a drop-in client-side feature.

B

Backend required

Requests are server-to-server, with OAuth 2.0, account linking, HMAC nonce verification, rate limits, and a publicly reachable webhook endpoint.

C

Privacy remains yours

Ring’s TAKE encryption changes the cloud story, but clips Muse already fetched and stored are governed by Muse’s own retention policy.

The upgrade path

From polling to webhooks.

Today’s unofficial event path polls. A real partner integration could use webhooks for motion and lifecycle events, which is architecturally cleaner and lower-latency—but it introduces partner approval and hosted-backend work.

That belongs in a future deployment tier, not in a template that promises a button and calls it done.

TAKE, plainly

Better encryption is not magic.

Keys may be deleted after a window, but if a user later watches old footage or uses smart features, keys can be sent back to the server. The field-guide conclusion is simple: do not call it end-to-end encryption, and do not use it as a reason to skip your own retention policy.
