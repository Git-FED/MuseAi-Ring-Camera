The short version

Useful, not magical.

Muse’s value is in checking the things Ring already lets it reach. The big boundary is direct live view, plus the practical costs of plans, battery throttling, unofficial APIs, and cloud dependency.

The pick

Battery 2K + Solo

For the lowest-friction setup: Battery Doorbell 2K at $99.99, plus Ring Solo at $4.99/month for recorded clips. If Muse will poll snapshots often, wired is the calmer choice.

The boundary

Live view is a separate problem.

Ring’s normal third-party path does not hand Muse a direct live stream. Home Assistant and ring-mqtt can bridge some of that gap, but the result is on-demand, cloud-routed, and not a CCTV replacement.
